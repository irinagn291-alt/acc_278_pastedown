Three behaviours are deliberate and can look unfinished on a short pass. Please try these first.

1. A cold launch can sit on a blank screen for a few seconds. A small spinner may appear with no caption. That pause is startup, not a hung launch. On a device with no saved work, three pages appear over it. Skip is at the top trailing corner. Continue is at the bottom. Continue on page 3, or Skip on any page, dismisses them.
2. After Paste adds a strip, the last line often stays selected. Paste can remain enabled if another volume could land, but a second tap refuses, leaves the board unchanged, and writes a clash. The Same-volume pastes refused count rises. VoiceOver says "Same volume. The foot keeps its line." Tap a chip from another volume, then Paste.
3. Volumes, Settings, and Adjacent volume ban have no Close. Drag the sheet down. Cuttings has Close. Sealed centos has Close and Back to the board.
